# myHabbit 12.8.0 — Teddy Room geometry & tactile studio

This update fixes two issues reported after 12.7.0: the room perspective looked crooked and the live purchase/preview workshop was awkward to scroll on touch screens.

## Teddy Room
- Coherent 2.5D room shell with aligned back wall, side walls, ceiling and floor.
- Staircase now connects to the upper landing instead of cutting through the scene.
- Window, door, fireplace, Teddy and upgrade hotspots were repositioned to the same visual perspective.
- Starter room remains intentionally sparse so upgrades visibly build the space over time.

## Live studio
- The room remains visible while editing.
- Category tabs and style cards support momentum horizontal swiping, scroll snapping and larger touch targets.
- The whole workshop can scroll vertically on smaller screens.
- Previewing a style preserves carousel position after re-rendering.
- Light haptic feedback is used for previews/categories and medium feedback for confirming an equip/purchase when haptics are enabled.
