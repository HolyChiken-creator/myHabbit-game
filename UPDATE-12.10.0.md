# myHabbit 12.10.0

## Teddy Room
- Removed the floating `+` upgrade markers from the room scene. Renovation now starts only from the Renovate control, so the scene stays clean.
- Simplified the room architecture into one coherent cozy room without the awkward staircase/balcony composition.
- Added a protected visual zone around Teddy and rebalanced the seat, table, storage, fireplace, plants, wall art and small props around the character.
- Normalized mobile scaling so furniture frames Teddy instead of covering him.

## Live renovation studio
- Made the studio more transparent and less visually destructive to the scene.
- The panel can be dragged by its top grip and resized from the lower-right handle. Its size/position is remembered.
- Added a reset-position button.
- Fixed scrolling: the whole panel can scroll vertically, category tabs scroll horizontally, and style cards are shown in a reachable grid (one column on narrow phones). Mouse-wheel horizontal scrolling is supported for horizontal controls.

## Admin decorator mode
- Added `Admin → Family and members → 🎨 Open decor`.
- Admin can unlock the entire Teddy Room catalog for any family member without level progression and without spending crystals. Existing equipped decor is preserved.
