myHabbit 12.6.0 update over 12.5.0

Primary changes:
- One persistent Teddy Room instead of room-background swapping.
- 15 separately upgradeable renovation zones.
- Sparse starter 2.5D room with stairs, upper railing, door, window and fireplace architecture.
- Four visible renovation stages: Bare room, Getting cozy, Warm home, Dream room.
- Sequential zone tiers: higher upgrades require the previous tier.
- Room progress tracks highest unlocked tiers and never falls when an older visual choice is equipped.
- Suggested next renovation steps in the room shop.
- Transparent furniture assets used as separate room objects.
- Mobile keeps the same room composition with scaled/simplified props.
- Crystal economy from 12.5.0 remains the funding source for renovation.
- Core room furniture is now included in offline PWA preload.
