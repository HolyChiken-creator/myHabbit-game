myHabbit 12.7.0 update over 12.6.0

Primary changes:
- Replaced the blocking Teddy Room decoration modal with a non-blocking live studio.
- Desktop studio sits beside the room; mobile/tablet studio sits below it so Teddy remains visible.
- Tapping any room style previews it instantly in the real Teddy Room.
- Preview is temporary: it never spends crystals and never mutates the saved room state.
- Explicit Buy & Equip / Equip confirmation is required before saving a style.
- Revert Preview restores the currently equipped room immediately.
- Closing the studio discards any temporary preview.
- Locked higher tiers can still be previewed, but purchase remains blocked until the previous tier is unlocked.
- Owned styles can be previewed and re-equipped without another charge.
- Added a visible Live Preview badge inside the room.
